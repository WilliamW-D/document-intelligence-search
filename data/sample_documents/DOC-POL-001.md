# Enterprise Data Retention & Archival Policy
Category: Policy
Author: Compliance & Legal Division
Published: 2025-01-15

## 1. Scope & Purpose
This policy defines mandatory requirements for retaining, archiving, and purging enterprise electronic data, financial records, customer personal information, and system telemetry across all corporate data repositories.

## 2. Retention Schedule Guidelines
Electronic communications and email logs must be retained for a minimum of seven (7) years from date of creation. Customer transaction histories and billing records must be preserved for ten (10) years in immutable cold storage to comply with statutory audit regulations.

## 3. Data Archival & Purging Procedures
Upon reaching the end of the mandatory retention period, non-record temporary data shall be automatically purged using cryptographically secure erasure methods. Data custodians must perform quarterly audits to verify compliance.
