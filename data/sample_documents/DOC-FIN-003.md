# Financial Travel Expense & Reimbursement Guidelines
Category: Finance
Author: Corporate Finance & Accounting
Published: 2025-03-01

## 1. Business Travel Meal Allowances
Employees traveling on authorized company business are eligible for meal expense reimbursement. The maximum daily allowance for travel meals is capped at $75 USD per day ($15 Breakfast, $25 Lunch, $35 Dinner). Itemized receipts are mandatory for all individual expenses exceeding $25 USD.

## 2. Lodging & Transportation Policies
Standard hotel accommodations must be booked through the corporate travel portal at pre-approved commercial rates. Rental cars require manager pre-approval and must be compact or mid-size vehicles.

## 3. Expense Report Submission Deadline
Expense reports must be submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of returning from business travel. Late expense filings require written authorization from the Department Vice President.
